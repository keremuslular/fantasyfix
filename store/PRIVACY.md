# FantasyFix privacy policy

FantasyFix does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- It does not read the content of web pages, cookies, passwords or form data.
- It does not use analytics or tracking of any kind.
- It makes no network requests of its own and sends nothing to the developer or anyone else.

The only thing it does is change the address of the ESPN Fantasy website's requests to ESPN's stats API, from `site.api.espn.com` to `site.web.api.espn.com`. Those requests go to ESPN exactly as they would without the extension, and are covered by ESPN's own privacy policy.

FantasyFix is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ESPN.
