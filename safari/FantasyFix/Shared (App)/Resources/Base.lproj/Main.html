Fixes missing matchups, game status and stats on ESPN Fantasy in Safari.

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → FantasyFix and turn it on.
  2. Under Permissions, set espn.com (or All Websites) to Allow.
  3. Open fantasy.espn.com in Safari.

You can turn on FantasyFix’s extension in Safari Extensions preferences.
